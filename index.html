Dear Keziah,

A very happy birthday to you. I’m honestly so overwhelmed and I don’t even know where to begin. Firstly, I would like to thank God for protecting you and keeping you safe for all these years. Us crossing paths was genuinely one of the greatest things to ever happen to me. I’m so grateful for our friendship and for everything we’ve been through together.

Thank you for being a friend, a sister, a safe place, and everything all in one. Thank you for being the friend I can talk to about anything without ever having to fear being judged. Thank you for listening to me, understanding me, and knowing me in ways that not many people do. Thank you for being my strength on the days I was weak. Thank you for guiding me when I went astray and for reminding me when I was losing my way. Thank you for defending me in rooms where I wasn’t there to defend myself. Thank you for always being someone I could count on.

I’m genuinely so grateful for you.

And I’m so proud of the woman you’re becoming. You’ve come so far, Keziah, and I hope you never forget that. I’m so proud of how strong you’ve been, of all the milestones you’ve achieved, and of all the times you kept going even on the days when you felt like you couldn’t.

There were days when things weren’t easy, but somehow you still stood strong. And I hope you realise how much strength that takes. I hope you know that you deserve to be proud of yourself too.

My prayer for you is that God continues to protect you, guide you and keep you. I pray that He gives you wisdom in every decision you make and gives you strength whenever life gets difficult. I pray that He opens the right doors for you and leads you towards everything He has planned for your life. I pray that your dreams come true, that you continue to grow into the woman you're meant to be, and that you never lose yourself along the way.

Jeremiah 29:11 says,

“For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the Lord, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.”

And I really pray that you hold onto that as you enter this new chapter. You might not know exactly what the future looks like, but God does. Trust Him with it.

I also pray that you always remember Philippians 1:6, that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion. I just want you to know how grateful I am that God allowed our paths to cross all those years ago.

We've grown up together, we've been through so much together, and you've become so much more than just a friend to me. You're family. You're my sister from another mother, and I genuinely don't know what my life would look like without all the experiences we've shared.

I’m so proud of you, I’m so grateful for you, and I’m so excited to see where life takes you. May God bless you abundantly, protect you always, and make this new chapter of your life one of your best yet.

Love Always,
Maria
